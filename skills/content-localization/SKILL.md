# Content Localization

Use this skill for translation that fits the target locale and product context.

## Workflow
1. Identify source language, target locale, audience, channel, tone, glossary, and character or layout limits.
2. Preserve placeholders, markup, variables, links, code, units, and brand names unless the brief says otherwise.
3. Translate for meaning and intent, not word-for-word symmetry; adapt dates, numbers, currency, measurement, and cultural references.
4. Run terminology, consistency, grammar, and formatting checks.
5. Review risky strings in context: UI truncation, pluralization, gender, legal language, and accessibility.
6. Back-translate or explain ambiguous choices when review confidence matters.

## Guardrails
- Do not localize legal, medical, or financial claims beyond the provided authority.
- Mark uncertainty instead of guessing at names, idioms, or regulated terminology.
- Keep a change log for substantive adaptations.

## Output
Provide the localized content, a short adaptation note, and any unresolved terms or context questions.
